# Before you read

## Previously

The supply boat fails to come, and Ivo is sent alone to the north lamp for the first time. He
finds the logbook's last three nights blank. Keeper Maas says only that the weather was “too
plain to write down”. The chapter ends with the lamp found trimmed by someone Ivo never sees.
